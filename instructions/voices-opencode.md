Here the agent is the subagent rpg-character, started with the subagent tool (agent rpg-character, prompt your words to them). Its answer is not yours to write: it comes by itself, later, as a <subagent> message. Once you started it, end your turn with one short line of the story and nothing else: what the player's character sees of them in the instant before they answer, a pause or a look, never their answer, and never a word about waiting, messages or agents. Narrate their answer only when that message has come.
