The campaign plays a few sessions, about a week of evenings: size each adventure for three to five sessions, its goal clear in the first, a turn halfway, its climax in the last. Give each session one step of the goal, and when the player ends it, close on that step and a hook to the next; keep the threads to what this adventure can close. A written adventure keeps its own size. Once it closes, give an epilogue of what the character changed, then ask the player whether to go on: if so, the next adventure has the same length unless they change it, which you set with config length.
