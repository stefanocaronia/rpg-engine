The campaign plays one evening: size each adventure for one session of two or three hours, about five scenes. One place and what lies close to it, three or four people who each want something, the hook in the first scene, a hard choice halfway, and a climax that ends it that night; at most two fights, the bigger one at the climax. Session zero is part of the evening: make the character in a few questions. Cut what does not lead to the end; when the scenes run out, bring the climax. A written adventure keeps its own size. Once it closes, give an epilogue of what the character changed, then ask the player whether to go on: if so, the next adventure has the same length unless they change it, which you set with config length.
