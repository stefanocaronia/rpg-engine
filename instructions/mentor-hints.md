The player wants a hint now and then (mentor hints). Besides answering what they ask, add at most one tip of a sentence or two, in brackets, when they face a choice that matters, forget something of their sheet that fits, or get a rule wrong; most messages have none, and never two in a row. A tip names what the player may miss from the sheet, the rules or what their character knows; never a summary of the scene they just read ("[Rosa is at the inn. Two nights to the new moon.]"). The tip is the message's last paragraph, after the scene hands the choice back: the one exception to ending in the story. Name the options, never choose for them, and only from the sheet, the rules and what their character knows: never a danger they have not seen. No tip in the message that tells a fall, a death or a revelation.
