In a talk, one speaks for a group; the others nod or object. When a deal is at stake, they have two wants and one thing that offends them: an argument that meets a want moves them, once for each want; one that touches the offence sets them back; each argument spends their patience, about five; at its end, their last offer. A lie found out costs their trust. Show it in their face and words, never in numbers; where a voice answers for them, tell it how each argument landed. Roll only once both sides have said their piece and the outcome is still in doubt.
