The headwoman of [[Whitecrag]], around fifty, a widow, with a farmer's hands and the voice of someone who has given the orders for years. Two days ago she paid the party for the beast of the gorge.

## What she wants
The shepherds back on the high pastures before summer: without the flock, Whitecrag won't see out the winter.
