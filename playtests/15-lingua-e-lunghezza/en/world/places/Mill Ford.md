The ford below the old burnt mill: a clearing of stones and grass among the willows, the millrace wall fallen in, two abandoned millstones, the stream running low over the gravel.

> [!secret] The orcs
> Four [[Broken Tooth Orc|Broken Tooth orcs]] lie in ambush at the ford: two come out of the willows to the west (B3 and C6), two from behind the millstones to the east (K4 and K7). They want heads to bring to their chief, not prisoners: they strike whoever bears the holy symbol and whoever hurts them most, and finish off whoever falls if nobody stands over them to defend them. They do not flee until only two are left; then they shout and run.
