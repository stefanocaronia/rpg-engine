# 19 La sera di Alessia: a real player, from the install to the end of an adventure

T-184. Alessia, a beta tester who does not program, plays one evening (2–3 hours) with Stefano beside her explaining; the master is Claude Code with rpg-engine 0.14.0. She installs the engine, makes her campaign and her character, installs the adventure a friend gave her and plays it to its end. What to ask her and what to watch on her side: [osservare.md](osservare.md), Sara's. This file and the folder never go to Alessia: she gets only a copy of `adventure/`.

## The adventure

`adventure/`, «L'ultima corsa del traghetto» 1.0.0, original, in Italian, CC BY 4.0, for 1–2 characters, one evening: `adventure.yaml` and 14 pages in `world/` (1 topic, the opening; 3 places; 5 characters; 2 items; 2 events, planned; 1 quest, planned). A ferry house on a flooding river, the last crossing at 23:30, a kitchen girl falsely accused of stealing a necklace, the steward who took it, the merchant who bought it, the ferrywoman who saw. It needs no system and says no levels, so it installs in `free` and in the 5e alike; its difficulties are words, with the 5e DC in brackets. Its dates are day 1, 21:30 and 23:30, counted from the day it is installed; the first scene is at 20:00. Its start, Riva della Livra, is in no place of the world: the master puts it there.

## The system

Proposal: `free`, outcomes `rolled`, a d6: her character in her own words in ten minutes instead of an hour of sheet, one die with the number said before, and the evening goes to the story. The 5e at 1st level if Alessia already plays D&D and wants it; Stefano decides before the evening.

## The evening

1. The install, from the guide for a friend on Windows (T-185), on her computer.
2. The setup of a new campaign, her choices: Stefano explains, never chooses. Companions: none suggested, one character; the adventure has five people to meet already.
3. The character, with the master.
4. Stefano gives her the folder or a zip of `adventure/`, renamed as she likes, and she asks the master in her words, as a friend would: «Un amico mi ha passato un'avventura…».
5. The play, about 90–120 minutes, to one of its endings.

Budget: 30' install, 20' setup and character, the rest to play. If the install eats the evening, that is the finding: stop, and play another time.
