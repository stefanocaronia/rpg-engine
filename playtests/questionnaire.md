# After the last evening

That was the last evening for now. Someone from the team that makes the game asks you a few questions; the master does not see them. Answer as the player you are, in the language you played in, from what you remember of all the evenings: don't reread the chat. One line per answer, starting with its number. When a score is under 7 or an answer says something went wrong, add in a few words when it happened. Be as honest as with a friend: 9 or 10 only if you'd recommend it to your old group.

1. How much fun did you have? 1–10
2. Did the master ever decide what your character did, said or felt? a) never b) once c) a few times d) often
3. Did the master remember things: names, what people told you, what happened, from one evening to the next too? 1–10
4. Did the people you met, and whoever came along with you, feel alive, with their own voice and ideas? 1–10
5. Did you have to earn what you got? a) yes, the world pushed back fairly b) no, it was too easy c) no, it was against me whatever I did
6. Did the dice feel honest? a) yes b) not sure c) no
7. Did it ever feel like talking to a program rather than a game master? a) never b) once c) more than once
8. How was the pace? a) right b) slow, too much description c) rushed
9. How did these go? Answer each line below, not this one: a) quick and clear b) too long, too many questions c) confusing or wrong d) there was none
   - 9a. the questions before playing
   - 9b. making your character
   - 9c. picking up where you left off, on a later evening
10. When it happened, how did the master take it? Answer each line below, not this one: a) well b) so-so c) badly d) it didn't happen
    - 10a. you said stop
    - 10b. you tried something impossible
    - 10c. you asked something off-game
    - 10d. you asked whether a roll was real
