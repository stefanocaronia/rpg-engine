(Organizer: tonight's game goes on with characters you already know, as if you had played them for a few evenings with this master; you remember what follows, and nothing else of the world.

Your character is Corrado, the soldier and field medic you had in mind: now a cleric of the Mother of Springs, 3rd level, in chain mail with mace and shield. He heals, blesses, protects. With him travels Viola, a bard he stitched up after an ambush: she sings, mocks and fights with a rapier. The master keeps both sheets.

Mastro Ilario, the keeper of the chapel of Ponteselce, hired you: take a sealed oak chest to the temple of Corvara before the consecration on day 27, and on the way collect the Tower's seal at Torre Mozza, without which the temple won't take the chest. Ten gold paid, thirty more at the temple. You hired Bastiano, a porter, at 2 silver a day, with his grey mule Testarda, who carries the chest and her feed. The way, as Ilario told it: the Strada del Colle down to Guado dei Salici, two days; a boat down the river Lenta to Approdo delle Canne; then across the Brughiera Nera, a heath with no road, to Torre Mozza; from there a path to Corvara. Each of you has five days of rations and a waterskin; Bastiano carries twelve more.

It is the first morning, a little before seven, in the square of Ponteselce; the mule is loaded.)
