# Moves

A move is what a character does when the story calls for a roll: roll the action die, add a stat, and compare the total with the two challenge dice.

## Adventure Moves

### Face Danger

When you attempt something risky or react to a threat, roll with the stat that fits how you act. On a strong hit you succeed; on a weak hit you succeed at a cost; on a miss the danger strikes.

### Gather Information

When you search an area, ask questions or follow a trail, roll with wits. On a strong hit you learn something that helps and take momentum.

## Combat Moves

### Strike

When you have initiative and attack in close quarters, roll with iron; at range, roll with edge.

***Momentum.*** A strong hit lets you keep initiative.
