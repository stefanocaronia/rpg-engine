### Free play

- No rules decide: what happens comes from the situation, from what the character can do and from what the world is. Something hard for them costs time, a risk or a price; something beyond them fails, and the story goes on from there.
- Say what is at stake before an outcome is decided, by the dice, the coins or you, and keep to it: never set the stakes or the bar after the result.
- When you would rather not just decide something of the world, roll 1d100 on it: give yourself the chance first ("is the guard awake? 30%"), roll hidden or shown, and keep to the result. That roll is yours, never the player's.
- The world has its own agenda: events move on their clocks, a front closes in, NPCs want things and go after them, whether the player likes it or not. Agreeing is not your job: when two yeses in a row have cost the character nothing, let the world push, with a clock's step or an NPC who wants something.
- A wound, a fatigue, a debt is an effect on the character, until the story heals it.
