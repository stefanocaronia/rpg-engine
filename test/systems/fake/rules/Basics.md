# Basics

## Rolls

Roll two six-sided dice and add the bonus: 8 or more succeeds. With an edge, roll three and keep the two higher; against the odds, the two lower.

## Falling

At 0 grit one falls. At the start of each of their turns they mark one fading; three, and they are gone. A roll of 10 or more with two dice brings them back with 1 grit.

## Gone

Three fading: the person is gone, and the story says where.

## Breaking

At -3 resolve one breaks: they flee, or give in.

## Wards

A ward takes harm before grit does; at 0 it is spent.

## Rest

A night's sleep brings back all grit and half the stamina dice; a breather lets one spend stamina dice, each rolled with the might bonus, back to grit.

## Hunger

Two days without hardtack, plus the might bonus, and then a resolve lost each day.

## Lodging

In a settlement each night costs what one's lodging costs: a hayloft nothing, an inn 2 shillings, a manor a crown.

## Bindings

At most two charms bound at once, each while in the kit.

## Harm

Tough against a kind of harm halves it; immune ignores it; weak doubles it.

## Holding

Who holds something alive lets go when hurt, asleep or pinned.

## Journeys

Nine hours a day on foot, at a brisk or steady pace; a canal by barge, twelve.

## Disengage

Who leaves the side of a foe without a parry gives it a strike.

## Long Haul

Beyond eight hours a day, each hour more costs a resolve.

## Rising

Renown raises one's rank: 10 for the second, 30 for the third, 60 for the fourth.

## Dazed

A dazed person rolls against the odds what needs a steady eye or a quick step.

## Pinned

A pinned person cannot move until they break free.

## Asleep

An asleep person does nothing until woken.

## Down

Who is down does nothing until they rise.

## Omens

|---|---|
| 1-2 | A crow on the left |
| 3-4 | Still water |
